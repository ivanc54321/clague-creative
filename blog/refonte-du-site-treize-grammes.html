Redesigning the Clague Creative Website

Author

Francis Méano

Brand designer

Chapter 1
Chapter 2
Chapter 3
Chapter 4
Chapter 5

This article is meant to be timeless, so let me set the context. It is late September 2024, and in three days we are launching our brand-new website after six months of intensive work. Here is an honest breakdown of the reasons that compelled us to undertake this redesign, the methodology we established, and the challenges we tackled along the way.

As a quick aside: had we been able to bypass this extra workload, we certainly would have, as our client roadmap was already overflowing! But this overhaul had become an absolute strategic necessity.

What pushed us to take the leap?

Our offering has evolved

Yet our public messaging had lagged behind, causing friction and missed opportunities. Classic client quote: "Oh, you build websites too? If only I had known, we would have done it with you..."

Our clients have changed

Between the early solo founders when Clague Creative started and today’s scale-ups, institutions, and enterprise SMEs, expectations have matured and competition is fierce. Direct feedback spoke volumes: "You look too small", "You seem too creative", "You're too expensive", "You're the 6th agency I've met—what truly makes you different?" In short: a glaring mismatch between our stature and our brand voice.

Our ambition

Undoubtedly our most decisive motivator. The ambitious clients slipping away—we want them. The flagship projects—we want them. We want to scale, double and triple our revenue, recruit top-tier talent, amplify Clague Creative's authority, and supercharge acquisition. In short: take Clague Creative to the highest tier.

‍

For all these reasons, on February 10, 2024, we sat down together to forge our strategic battle plan.

‍

The strategic milestones

Step 1: Questioning our vision

Returning to fundamentals—the "why" behind Clague Creative. A vital exercise to re-energize the team and align executive vision. True to our roots, our mission remains steadfast: empower ambitious innovators and grow alongside them until we become the brand behind the brands.

‍

Step 2: Defining short-term objectives to achieve this vision

In our case, the mandate was crystal clear: expand visibility and authority, reassure enterprise organizations of our capability, and accelerate growth.

  • Visibility = Presence
  • Authority = Recognition
  • Growth = Business Performance

‍

Step 3: Listing and prioritizing strategic action items

To boost visibility, we committed to a robust SEO strategy and active authority-building across social platforms (lots of work!).

For brand authority, the foundation was already solid: award-winning projects, international press features, industry accolades, and outstanding client reviews. The goal was making all of this shine front-and-center, complemented by expert thought-leadership on our blog.

Finally, for growth: continue expanding our ecosystem, generate inbound leads, and re-engage alumni clients. We conceived a dedicated client portal to maintain ongoing proximity with partners.

‍

Step 4: Brand Platform

‍Down to work: defining our brand universe.

The team gathered to articulate our comprehensive brand platform: vision, mission, promise, values, and brand tone. Deceptively simple in theory, remarkably rigorous in execution. It demands ruthless objectivity.

You must identify what sets you apart. The greatest mirror of objectivity is your clients. Ask them why they chose you and what stands out in your partnership. In our case, the unanimous answers were active listening, deep involvement, curiosity, and the ability to solve bottom-line business problems. Through this exact lens, we formulated our brand foundation.

"Mark your market without detour: power business growth through brand design and deliver concrete business impact to organizations tired of empty corporate fluff."

Step 5: Content Strategy & Information Architecture

At this stage, we map the user journey and customer friction points. We address and dismantle every hesitation within an intentional narrative flow across each service page, ensuring seamless pathways to conversion.

‍

‍

Step 5 (cont.): Content Architecture and Commercial Narrative

To maintain razor-sharp focus, we structured service offerings and wireframed the site architecture:

  1. A homepage introducing Clague Creative and its unique positioning.
  2. A core service page dedicated to Brand Strategy & Creation.
  3. A service page dedicated to turnkey Website & Web App Design.
  4. An activation page dedicated to integrated Digital Campaigns.

Next, we cataloged our core value propositions, prioritizing over thirty talking points against three criteria:

  1. Alignment with brand positioning.
  2. Relevance to audience needs.
  3. Demonstrable proof (audiences believe evidence, not promises).

This paved the way for conversion-driven copywriting and narrative cadence.

‍

Step 6: Content Narrative Refinement

At this stage, we map the user journey and customer friction points. We address and dismantle every hesitation within an intentional narrative flow across each service page, ensuring seamless pathways to conversion.

‍

Step 7: Visual Concept & Web Design

The objective: deliver a singular web experience bold enough to earn international acclaim (Awwwards, FWA), yet pragmatic enough to convert enterprise prospects.

It took four intensive months of creative exploration and internal debates. Yet we rallied behind a high-craft concept deeply intertwined with our brand identity.

Step 8: Development on Webflow

‍ Development on Webflow

For this milestone, we partnered with Thomas Carré, a true creative technologist. We worked hand-in-hand fine-tuning micro-interactions, GSAP timelines, and performance optimizations.

Our challenge: build an agile, scalable digital playground capable of evolving with our rapid growth!

...If you are reading this note, you have explored our live digital platform. What do you think? :)

‍

The Takeaways

Even with deep industry experience and craft maturity, working on one’s own brand is notoriously demanding. Maintaining emotional detachment and objective discipline while avoiding de-prioritizing internal work over client deadlines requires immense resolve.

We relish audacious challenges and do not regret a minute, but it is certainly a marathon we do not recommend undertaking unprepared :) !

‍

What comes next? What to expect

‍

This is where the real journey begins!

We are leveraging this milestone to announce our new offerings, enhanced scope, and forward momentum to partners and networks.

1. 1. This launch announcement generates high curiosity and invites exploration of our newly expanded service capabilities.

2. 2. We will sustain momentum with continuous announcements highlighting our growth trajectory (team additions, feature rollouts, case study drops).

3. 3. Immediate traffic will bolster organic domain authority, search engine presence, and brand reputation.

4. 4. Multi-channel distribution of expert content across LinkedIn will continually drive high-intent founders and executives to our platform.

5. 5. An exclusive client hub will centralize brand assets and guidelines for active clients, driving recurring engagement.

6. Finally, we will continuously optimize Core Web Vitals, conversion architecture, and international SEO. Our ambition: make this website an unstoppable inbound engine.

‍

In short: we can’t wait to share our milestones with you!
