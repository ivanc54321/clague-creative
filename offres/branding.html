Do meetings with you include coffee and croissants?

Of course! Whenever possible, we invite you to our studio or travel to your headquarters for kickoff and strategy workshops. In-person collaboration builds unmatched trust and clarity. Subsequent reviews and design sprints are smoothly handled via video conferencing and collaborative tools.

How long does a brand identity last?

Generally, a well-engineered brand identity lasts 5 to 7 years before needing iteration or evolution, depending on market shifts and business expansion. A professional branding engagement is an enduring strategic asset.

Where can I see examples of your deliverables?

We would love to walk you through sample deliverables and in-depth case studies during a 30-minute discovery call. Contact us, let us know your industry, and we will showcase relevant work tailored to your challenges.

What exact deliverables do you provide and in what formats?

Chronologically, we first deliver a strategic Brand Foundation summarizing market context, vision, mission, brand values, and positioning. Next come 2-3 creative design routes (40-60 pages), followed by an extensive interactive Brandbook and design token repository. All vectors, assets, layouts, and guidelines are organized in a dedicated Figma workspace and shared cloud drive.

What does the first phase of the collaboration look like?

The first phase is an intensive Brand Strategy Workshop (2 to 4 hours) where we uncover your core narrative, audience psychographics, and business positioning. We synthesize these findings into a strategic Brand Guide before opening any visual design tools.

Who will be working on my project?

You partner directly with agency founders and lead designers from day one to final delivery. No junior account managers—only dedicated, senior creative directors and specialists across strategy, typography, motion, and digital execution.

How much does the brand offering cost?

Our brand creation and rebranding engagements are tiered (Light / Regular / Heavy) to match your scope and growth stage. Custom modules (e.g. specialized physical signage, packaging architecture) can be tailored to your requirements. Schedule a discovery call for detailed estimates and scope options.
