Was the Clague Creative agency website fast to build?

Not at all! Building for oneself is always the hardest creative challenge, but we hope you enjoy every micro-interaction and technical detail ;).

Which tools and technologies do you use?

For concept architecture: our minds + FigJam. For design: Figma alongside Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, Photoshop, After Effects). For integration: Webflow, GSAP, and modern JavaScript. For web applications: Wized, Supabase, or custom frontend integrations. This standard accelerates delivery while ensuring pixel perfection.

What types of websites do you create?

We specialize in turnkey corporate marketing websites, brand portfolio platforms, and interactive web applications. We do not build mass e-commerce storefronts, but for modern digital brand experiences, we push creative and technical boundaries to make your site a true conversion powerhouse.

Are your websites optimized for SEO and performance?

Yes. Semantic HTML5, structured metadata, mobile-first responsiveness, and optimal asset compression are baked into every build. Our projects achieve industry-leading Core Web Vitals and search rankings.

How much does a website cost?

Our engagements range from €3,500 for focused landing pages/MVPs to €7,500–€12,500 for ambitious 5-10 page brand websites, and €12,500–€23,000+ for large corporate sites with custom CMS structures and web applications. Schedule a call for a tailored scope and precise quote.

How long does it take to create a website with Clague Creative?

Timelines typically range between 6 and 12 weeks depending on scope, copy production, custom interactions, and CMS complexity.

How many team members work on my website?

A complete multidisciplinary squad covers your project: brand strategist, copywriter, UI/UX art director, motion designer, and Webflow developer. You collaborate directly with a founding partner who coordinates execution flawlessly.
